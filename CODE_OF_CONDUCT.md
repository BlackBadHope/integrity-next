# Code of Conduct

We want participation in Integrity Guardian to be safe, constructive and
welcoming.

Be respectful, discuss technical decisions with evidence, accept correction,
and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing or disclosure of private
information. Disagreement is expected; personal attacks are not.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Reports concerning
maintainer conduct may be submitted through GitHub private vulnerability
reporting when no other private contact is available.

Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, repair and repeated
behavior. This policy applies to project spaces and public representation of the
project.
